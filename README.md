# Financial Business Rules & Requirements

A Business Analyst portfolio repository demonstrating how financial business rules are captured, validated, and traced from policy intent to implementable requirements.

## Purpose

This repository showcases BA artifacts for **business rules management** in retail banking and payments contexts: eligibility logic, fee calculation, regulatory thresholds, and exception handling — expressed as decision tables, BRDs, and traceable user stories.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Business rules decomposition (decision tables, rule catalogs)
- BRD authoring with measurable acceptance criteria
- Requirements traceability (policy → rule → story → test)
- Cross-functional stakeholder alignment (Risk, Compliance, Engineering)
- Gap analysis between manual policy interpretation and automated rules
- Requirements quality review, with findings closed through change requests
- Worked numeric scenarios, edge case registers, and boundary-level UAT plans

## Disclaimer

All scenarios, amounts, and entity names are **fictional** and intended for portfolio demonstration only.
